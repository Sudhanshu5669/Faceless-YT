# Niche scan, 2026-11-25 (high demand, low supply)

Method (Steffen Miro's test): for each niche, the top 12 YouTube search results with views, channel subscribers,
upload date. Signals: **breakouts** = channels under 100k subs with 50k+ views and more views than subs;
**supply pressure** = results from 100k+ / 1M+ channels. 27 searches, 324 videos. Scripts and raw JSON are in the
session scratchpad (`niche_scan.py`, `niche_score.py`); one search per niche, so it's a sample, not a census.

## Notes

- The zombie survival channels are stick-figure / doodle animation (StickFallout, DoodleSurvivals, Mr. DoomStick,
  Stick Survives): cheap to make, so copycats will flood in fast (Steffen's warning). Quality is the moat.
