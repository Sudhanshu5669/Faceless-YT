# 41 Workers Trapped Inside a Mountain: script draft v1

*Drafted 2026-11-15 for the user to edit. Beats 1-3 are the built hook (waiting for verdicts).
Beats 4-28 are new: nothing is built for them yet. About 330 words, roughly 2:10 at the
narrator's pace. Every fact below has a source at the bottom; lines marked **CHECK** rest on a
single report or a detail I could not confirm, so verify or cut them before recording.*

Tone: third person to match the hook (the house "Imagine you're…" opening would make the viewer
one of 41 real men; ask the user). Respectful about the disaster, light irreverence only toward
the machines. The real people (rescuers, rat-hole miners, Vakeel Hasan) are toon actors, not
caricatures of their faces, unless the user wants caricatures.

## Sources

- Date, 05:30 IST, the 4.5 km Silkyara Bend–Barkot tunnel, the 60 m blockage about 200 m in,
