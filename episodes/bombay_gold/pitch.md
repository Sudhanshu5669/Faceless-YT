# Pitch: the day it rained gold on Bombay

Short (9:16, ~120-150 s), true story. Facts: `research.md` (numbers in brackets refer to its table).
Tone: grand and spectacular, but with restraint: 800+ people died. No jokes about the dead; the spectacle
is the explosion and the gold, the heart is the 66 firemen.

## A. "The Day It Rained Gold" (recommended)

- **Title:** The Day It Rained Gold on Bombay
- **Hook (0-5 s), cold open:** a quiet Girgaum home, 1944. A gold bar smashes through into the room.
  "In 1944, gold fell from the sky over Bombay. This is where it came from."
- **Escalation:** the ship docks with gold, cotton and 1,400 tons of explosives [3-5] → smoke at 12:30 [7] →
  firemen and fireboats fight it [8] → 16:06, the blast: the ship splits, a 3,857-ton ship lands on the quay,
  windows break 12 km away, the ground shakes in Shimla [9, 11, 15] → burning cotton and gold rain on the city
  [12, 21] → the second blast [10].
- **Turn:** the gold bar in Girgaum: the man hands it to the police, gets Rs 999 and gives it all to the
  victims [21].
- **Ending:** 66 firemen died, and that is why 14 April is India's Fire Service Day [18]. Bookend: 2011,
  a dredger in the same dock brings up a gold bar that has been lying there for 67 years [22]. Loop to the
  falling bar.
- **Grand set pieces:** the dock at full scale (ships, cranes, a crowd of dockworkers, haze); a smoke column
  over the city; the blast shockwave racing across Bombay's rooftops; a ship flung onto the quay; burning cotton
  and gold falling like rain at golden-hour light.

## B. "4:06 PM": the countdown

- **Title:** Why India's Firefighters Remember 4:06 PM
- **Hook:** a dock clock at 12:30. "You have three and a half hours. You don't know it yet."
- **Shape:** second person, you're a Bombay fireman. Each beat is a clock jump (12:30 smoke, 13:45 alarm,
  15:50 abandon ship, 16:06). The clock stops at 4:06 [9]. Then the gold rain as the shock reveal.
- **Ending:** the stopped clock, and Fire Service Day [18].
- **Strength:** tension and a clear spine. **Weakness:** the gold, the most clickable part, comes late.

## C. "The Floating Bomb"

- **Title:** How to Accidentally Blow Up a City
- **Hook:** "Step one: put the cotton under the bombs."
- **Shape:** the inquiry's list of mistakes as a checklist [25]: cotton below the munitions, no red flag,
  explosives kept on board. Each mistake is shown as a dockworker loading the ship. Then the price of each mistake.
- **Strength:** fresh and educational. **Weakness:** the irreverent tone sits badly with 800 deaths.

## Recommendation

**A, with B's clock as the spine:** the gold cold open, then the dock clock ticking through the afternoon to
4:06. That gives us the clickable hook, the tension and the respectful ending.
