## Conflicting: decide before use

- **Gold value:** £890,000 (W1, S1) vs £2 million (S2). → Don't say a value, or say "nearly a million pounds"
  only with W1/S1.
- **Bar weight:** 28 lb / 12.7 kg (W2, S2) vs 800 troy oz / ~25 kg (W1). → Avoid stating a weight.
- **Gold bar hit:** "through the wall" (S2) vs "through the roof" (AP). The finder is unnamed in S2 and named
  Burjor Cooverji Motiwalal in AP. → Say "crashed into his home" and don't name him.
- **Firemen:** 66 (W1 body, FS, the Fire Service Day dedication) vs 71 (W1 lead, Free Press Journal). → Use 66.
- **Injured:** 2,500-3,000 (W1) vs ~1,800 (S2). → Leave out.
- **"Everything that will burn or blow up":** W1 uses this to describe the cargo; the attribution to Naismith
  isn't confirmed. → Not a quote in narration.
- **How high/far the gold flew:** no source gives a distance for gold. → Show it falling over the city;
  don't give a number.
