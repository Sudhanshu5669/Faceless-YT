# "Kidnapped. Trapped in the Taj. Still Standing." Gautam Adani: script v2 (for approval)

Format: toon Short, 9:16, English narration, true story in the present tense (hook in the past tense).
Pace (from the Mr omi reference, 2026-11-26): brisk voice, about 165 words a minute, a new shot every 1.8 s or less.
Tone: fast and grand for the climb; restrained for the abduction and 26/11; neutral and exact for the cases.

Title options:
1. Kidnapped. Trapped in the Taj. Still Standing.
2. Gunmen Kidnapped Him. Then He Built an Empire.
3. The School Dropout Who Became Asia's Richest Man

`[n]` = planned shots for the line, separated by `/`. **K** = a Kimodo motion. **INSERT** = one giant object or number
on a plain textured stage.

## Script

**HOOK**

1. Gunmen once kidnapped Gautam Adani.
   *[3: night, a white car brakes in a headlight beam / his face fills the back window / low angle, two silhouettes
   step into the light]*
2. Today, he's one of the richest people in Asia.
   *[2: same face, calm, by day / crane down past him to a whole port working]*
3. And he never even finished school.
   *[2: a teenager walks out of a school gate / feet-only close-up as the gate clangs shut behind them]*

**THE CLIMB**

4. He's the seventh of eight children of a small cloth merchant.
   *[2: courtyard, children arrive one per word / the seventh turns to camera as the father unrolls cloth]*
5. At sixteen, he heads to Mumbai to sort diamonds.
   *[3: INSERT a giant `16` / he runs along a platform with a bag (K) / lamp close-up, a diamond flashes]*
6. Then his brother buys a plastic factory and calls him home.
   *[2: the phone rings among the diamonds / the brother waves him in beside the machine]*
7. The factory is hungry for raw plastic, so he starts importing it.
   *[3: the machine coughs to a stop / he hauls a cargo net on a rope (K) / the machine roars, film pours out]*
8. In nineteen eighty-eight, he starts his own trading firm.
   *[2: he hangs the "ADANI EXPORTS" board / low angle, he steps back, hands on hips]*
9. Then India opens its economy, and he jumps into metals, cloth, even farm produce.
   *[3: a barrier lifts / steel coils and cloth bales thunder past / top-down, grain sacks bury the street]*

**THE PORT**

10. But traded goods need a port.
    *[2: a cart of his goods races to the water / it stops dead at the edge, the stack wobbles]*
11. In nineteen ninety-five, he wins a contract to run one, at Mundra.
    *[3: grand empty wide of mudflats and sea / he drives a survey flag into the ground (K) / contact close-up, mud
    splashes]*
12. It starts with a single jetty.
    *[2: piles slam down one after another / one small ship noses up to one thin jetty]*
13. Today, it's India's largest private port.
    *[3: same frame, cranes rise / container stacks climb past the lens / night floodlights snap on, high wide]*

**THE TWO NIGHTS**

14. Then comes a night in January, nineteen ninety-eight.
    *[2: INSERT a calendar page, `JAN 1998` / the lit club door]*
15. He leaves a club, and gunmen take him and an associate.
    *[3: the two walk to the car / headlights block the road / car doors open, hard cut to black]*
16. A day later, both are free.
    *[2: INSERT a clock spinning a full day / dawn, two figures on an empty street]*
17. The men put on trial are eventually acquitted.
    *[2: courtroom, a file slides across the bench / close-up, the gavel falls; no faces in the dock]*
18. In two thousand eight, he's having dinner at Mumbai's Taj hotel.
    *[2: the Taj at night, grand / warm dining room, he lifts a cup]*
19. Then the attack begins.
    *[2: INSERT the cup trembling, huge / the chandelier sways and the lights cut]*
20. All night, he hides inside.
    *[2: a torch leads guests down a service corridor / he sits against the wall in the dark]*
21. Commandos get him out the next morning.
    *[2: boots in the corridor / a door opens to grey morning and a commando's hand]*
22. He later says he saw death just fifteen feet away.
    *[1: quiet close on his face, slow push. No gag]*

**THE EMPIRE**

23. He keeps building: power plants, a coal mine in Australia, Mumbai's airport, cement.
    *[5: he plants his feet (K) / cooling towers land / a mine excavator bites / an airport tower and plane / a
    cement works slams down]*
24. In twenty twenty-two, he passes Mukesh Ambani as Asia's richest man.
    *[2: two caricatures on rising platforms / Adani's shoots past]*
25. By August, only two people on Earth are richer.
    *[2: low angle up the platform into the clouds / INSERT a giant `3`]*

**THE FALL**

26. Five months later, one report lands.
    *[2: top-down, a thick report falls / it hits the desk, the whole tower shakes]*
27. A US short seller, Hindenburg Research, accuses his group of stock manipulation and fraud.
    *[3: the report cover / pages fan open / his face, shocked]*
28. The group fires back with a four hundred and thirteen page denial.
    *[2: he slaps down a stack taller than the report / INSERT counter to `413`]*
29. But the shares still crash. Over a hundred billion dollars in value, gone.
    *[3: a Dalal Street ticker plunges / the platform drops like a lift, he flails (K) / INSERT `$100 BILLION+`]*
30. So he cancels a fully subscribed twenty thousand crore share sale, and returns the money.
    *[3: investors queue at a counter / he hauls the shutter down (K) / bundles slide back across the counter]*
31. In twenty twenty-four, US prosecutors charge him over an alleged two hundred and fifty million dollar bribery
    scheme.
    *[4: a US federal courthouse, low angle / a sealed indictment tears open / INSERT `$250 MILLION` / stamp:
    `ALLEGED`. No cash, no handover]*
32. His group calls it baseless.
    *[1: a statement sheet, `BASELESS`]*

**THE TURN**

33. Then it turns. India's market regulator clears the group on key Hindenburg claims.
    *[3: the SEBI building / an order is stamped / the platform climbs again]*
34. In America, he agrees to pay six million dollars to settle a civil case, without admitting or denying wrongdoing.
    *[4: a US court door / INSERT a cheque, `$6 MILLION` / he signs / close on the line "without admitting or
    denying"]*
35. And a judge dismisses the criminal charges, while sharply criticising how the government dropped them.
    *[3: gavel falls, file closes / the judge frowns / a finger taps a page of the opinion]*

**LOOP**

36. A school dropout built all this. And none of it was certain, not on the night...
    *[3: high wide of the port at night / crane to the road beside it / a white car's headlights swing in, matching
    frame 1]*

## Choices and simplifications (check these)

- **"But" and "so" are used only where the link is sourced** (the factory led to the imports; the share fall led to the
  cancelled sale). No motive is given for the port, the abduction, the report or the charges.
- **Left out:** allegations of political favour (the group and the government deny them), the 2012 SFIO case,
  environmental fights over the Australian mine, the OFAC settlement, and charges against other US defendants that
  were still being litigated (the Justice Department went to the appeals court in Sept 2026). Critics' biggest
  theme, political favour, is therefore absent.
- **No ransom** is mentioned (reported, never established) and **no accused is named or drawn**.
- "All night, he hides inside": reports differ on kitchen, basement or toilet, so no spot is named.
- Line 22 is his own account, said as such.
- Line 2 says "one of the richest" because the exact rank moves.
- Before publishing: re-check the US and ranking facts on the upload date.
