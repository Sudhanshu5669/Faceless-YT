# Mangalyaan: beat sheet and shot list

Script: `script.md` (approved 2026-11-18). This file turns every line into shots: the place, the
gag (who does what to which object), what lands on which word, the faces, the camera and the sound.
Target: about 46 shots in about 150 s (mean about 3.2 s; hook and lists faster).

## Places (each one a gag script in `gags/`, far apart in one stage set)

| Gag | Place | Real details that make it read |

## Open choices (decide while building)

- Line 7: a chalk arc on the engineer's whiteboard, or an arc in the real sky over the pad. Try both as
  stills and keep the clearer one.
- The sanction letter (line 5) must not invent an official text: it shows only the mission name and an
  "APPROVED" stamp, not a fake order number or a signature.
- Tweet cards (line 18) are faithful recreations: Curiosity's profile name and handle, the reported text,
  the date.
