## Description

```
A one-kilometre auto ride in Ahmedabad costs about ten rupees. India went to Mars for seven.

In 2012, ISRO had never sent anything farther than the Moon, more than half of all missions to Mars had failed, and the next launch window was 15 months away. The budget: ₹450 crore, less than the Hollywood film Gravity. The PSLV couldn't throw a spacecraft to Mars, so ISRO swung it around Earth, loop after loop, and slung it out.

On 24 September 2014, after a 24-minute burn, partly behind Mars and out of contact, the Mars Orbiter Mission entered orbit: first in Asia, and the first country to reach Mars on its first try. Built to last six months, it kept working for almost eight years.
```
