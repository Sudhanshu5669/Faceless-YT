# How Mumbai Delivers Your Lunch by Train

A dramatized delivery along the documented Dadar–Churchgate route. One steel
tiffin stays in the frame through the handoffs. The painted mark is a prop
design for the story. The dabbawala system uses lid markings to route deliveries.

| Shot | Narration | Action and payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen | Imagine you're a lunchbox in Mumbai. You've got a train to catch. Your ticket is painted on your lid. | The cook puts a roti in the top tier, closes the clasp on “catch,” and tips the painted lid toward the window light on “ticket.” |
| Dadar lane | A dabbawala picks you up at the door. His bicycle is already carrying half the lane's lunch. | Match the handle across the handoff. The courier slides the tiffin into the last gap in his rack. The bicycle leans, then straightens as he rolls off. |
| Dadar sorting | At Dadar, the mark on your lid tells the sorter which station you're going to. You're bound for Churchgate. | A sorter turns two similar tiffins, reads the lids, and puts each with its destination group. Stay with ours. |
| Dadar platform | The local pulls in. You're riding in the luggage compartment, with everybody else's lunch. | The local brakes. Couriers lift the bundle together. Two tiffins touch once as the load swings and settles. |
| On the local | For a few stops, everybody gets to sit still. | Hold at tiffin height. Window light slides over the lids and the bundle sways on the rails. |
| Churchgate sorting | At Churchgate, you're sorted again. This time the code tells them which building and which floor. | Match the lift off the train to the set-down beside the station. A fingertip traces the markings and the tiffin joins the courier's cart. |
| Office | By lunchtime, you're on the right desk. And the roti from this morning is still right on top. | The recipient opens the lid in the same framing as the kitchen. Hold on the roti and the smile. |
| Return | After lunch, the dabbawala comes back for you. You've got another train to catch. | The worker closes the empty tiers and hands them back. The latch sound matches the opening for the loop. |

The kitchen shot uses a short cut of Nightflame's recorded metal clasp. The
bicycle, platform and onboard recordings are staged in `output/mumbai_lunch/sfx/`.
