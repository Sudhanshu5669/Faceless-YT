# In This Town, Nobody Is Allowed to Dream

Revised two-minute draft. Bracketed directions are visual and unspoken; timings are targets, pending recorded voice and editing.

## The rule · 0:00–0:18

In this town, nobody is allowed to dream.

If you do, a yellow dot appears above your head.

And the next morning, the king's soldiers take you to the castle on the hill.

Nobody ever comes back.

[Soldiers lead a dreamer away from his family. The castle watches over the town.]

But I dream every night. And I never get a dot.

## Maya · 0:18–0:32

In my dreams, I can fly. And every night, I meet my friend Maya.

She's an angel.

Once, I asked her, “Why don't I get a yellow dot?”

She smiled. “Don't worry about it.”

[She catches a falling star and places it in the narrator's hands. They sit together beneath an upside-down ocean.]

## The dot · 0:32–0:54

One night, rain wakes me. My little sister is asleep beside me.

I turn to wake her.

And there it is.

A yellow dot above her head.

I wrap a blanket around her and run. But the light shines right through.

[The siblings race through the rain. A soldier spots the glow. At the river, he catches the sister as the narrator reaches for her.]

I try to hold on to her.

But they pull her away.

She keeps calling my name as they take her toward the castle.

[The narrator reaches after her until she disappears into the rain.]

## The bargain · 0:54–1:10

I close my eyes and do what Maya taught me. I fall asleep while I'm still awake.

“Maya, please. They took my sister.”

She holds out her hand.

“I can save her. But you have to let me control your body.”

I look at her hand.

Then I take it.

## The truth · 1:10–1:30

I wake up. But I can't move.

My hand rises, and the street explodes.

Maya tears through the town. I beg her to stop.

She doesn't.

Her wings turn black.

Maya isn't an angel. She's a demon who came into our world through dreams.

She hid my dot. She was waiting for me to let her in.

[The star she gave the narrator darkens. In the dream world, Maya's hand closes around the hidden yellow light.]

The king knew dreamers could become vessels. The dots were made to find them.

Then the castle gates open.

Everyone he took walks out. Alive.

He trained them to bring dreams to life.

## The fight · 1:30–1:49

The dreamers attack. Maya tears through them.

Then I hear my sister.

“Please. Come back.”

[Inside the dream, the narrator grips Maya's hand. The island cracks. Maya is pulled free of their body.]

I pull her out. But she's still too strong.

The dreamers hold her with chains of light. My sister takes my hand.

Together, we dream.

[A sword of starlight forms. The siblings fly at Maya as she breaks the chains. They strike the star in her chest. It bursts into light.]

## A new morning · 1:49–2:00

The king sends the dreamers home and ends the ban.

That night, a yellow dot glows above my sister's head.

She smiles in her sleep.

This time, I let her dream.

[Yellow lights glow above the rooftops, all the way to the castle.]

## Timing

Target: 2 minutes. The chase, capture and betrayal have room for pauses; the battle is largely visual. Timing is provisional until narration is recorded and shots are edited.
