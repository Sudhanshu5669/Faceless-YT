# Chandrayaan-1 and Chandrayaan-2: research notes (2026-11-22)

Facts gathered for the pitch. Each row is checked against at least one source; "careful" rows
need wording care in the script. Script lines will cite these in `script.md`.

## Chandrayaan-1 (2008-2009)

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Launched 22 Oct 2008, 06:22 IST, PSLV-C11, Sriharikota; 1,380 kg | [ISRO](https://www.isro.gov.in/Chandrayaan_1.html), [NSSDC/ISRO release](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/text/chandrayaan1_pr_20081022.txt) |
| Cost ₹386 crore (dollar conversions vary, don't quote one) | [PIB 2003](https://archive.pib.gov.in/release02/lyr2003/rsep2003/11092003/r110920035.html) |
| Lunar orbit 100 km × 100 km; orbit insertion 8 Nov 2008 | ISRO, Wikipedia |
| Moon Impact Probe: a box with the Indian flag painted on its sides, released from the orbiter, ~25-min descent, hit near Shackleton crater (south pole) 14 Nov 2008, 20:31 IST; timed for Children's Day (Nehru's birthday) | [SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/?p=53412), [Planetary Society](https://www.planetary.org/articles/1740), [Wikipedia: MIP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon_Impact_Probe) |
| Careful: "Kalam's idea" for the MIP is weakly sourced; "4th/5th flag on the Moon" disputed. Leave both out. | — |
| NASA's Moon Mineralogy Mapper (M3), flying on Chandrayaan-1, found water/hydroxyl on the Moon's surface; announced 24 Sep 2009 (Science) | [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/chandrayaan-1/), [JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasa-funded-scientists-detect-water-on-moons-surface-that-hints-at-water-below/), [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2009-09-deep-impact-moon-mineralogy-mapper.amp) |
| Apollo sample boxes had leaked, so traces of water in Moon rocks were assumed to be Earth contamination | [Univ. of Tennessee (Larry Taylor), 2009](https://news.utk.edu/2009/09/24/new-research-shows-water-present-across-the-moons-surface) |
| Careful: ISRO says the MIP's CHACE also saw water (announced 25 Sep 2009, the day after NASA); NASA calls it inconclusive | [Planetary Society](https://www.planetary.org/articles/2430), NASA |
| Hotter than expected: ISAC director T K Alex: "We assumed the temperature at 100 km above the Moon's surface would be around 75 °C"; star trackers failed; orbit raised from 100 to 200 km, 16-19 May 2009, to cool it | [Physics Today ("Heat stroke killed Chandrayaan-1", citing TOI)](https://physicstoday.aip.org/news/heat-stroke-killed-chandrayaan-1), [NASA PDS mission catalog](https://pds.nasa.gov/data/pds3/backup/202006-img/zzold/missionCH1.cat) |
| Contact lost 29 Aug 2009 (01:30 IST); ISRO ended the mission 31 Aug; planned for 2 years, lasted ~10 months; ISRO: 95 % of objectives met; likely power-supply failure from overheating | [ESA](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Chandrayaan-1_loses_radio_contact), [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/chandrayaan-1/) |
| Lost, then found: NASA/JPL's 70 m Goldstone radar found it still orbiting the Moon on 3 Jul 2016 (announced Mar 2017), ~200 km above the poles, ~180° away from the 2009 prediction; ~1.5 m cube | [JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/new-nasa-radar-technique-finds-lost-lunar-spacecraft/), [Space.com](https://www.space.com/35999-lost-indian-moon-probe-found-radar.html) |
| Mangalyaan's body was a modified I-1K bus, the same bus flight-proven on Chandrayaan-1 | [ISRO: MOM spacecraft](https://www.isro.gov.in/Spacecraft.html) |

## Chandrayaan-2 (2019-)

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Cost ₹978 crore (₹603 cr spacecraft + ₹375 cr rocket); Avengers: Endgame ≈ $356 M ≈ ₹2,443 crore, so less than half; also less than Interstellar ($165 M) | [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/trends/chandrayaan-2-mission-cost-india-rs-978-crore-avengers-endgame-film-made-rs-2443-crore/story/366550.html), [Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/general/the-chandrayaan-2-missions-budget-is-less-than-half-of-the-budget-of-the-avengers-endgame-49159.html) |
| Rocket GSLV Mk III, nicknamed "Baahubali" | [Asian Age](https://www.asianage.com/science/160719/chandrayaan-2-launch-new-date-after-probe-by-experts.html) |
| 15 Jul 2019: 02:51 launch called off about an hour before (countdown held at T-56 min) for a technical snag; President Kovind was at Sriharikota; press reported a helium leak in the cryogenic stage (ISRO never named it); relaunched 22 Jul 2019, 14:43 | [ISRO](https://www.isro.gov.in/CHANDRAYAAN%20_2_LAUNCH_RESCHEDULED.html), [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/930520/scroll_in), [Hans India](https://thehansindia.com/news/national/chandrayaan-2-blast-off-halted-over-fuel-leak-reports-547039) |
| Orbiter + Vikram lander (named after Vikram Sarabhai) + Pragyan rover; target near the south pole | ISRO, Wikipedia |
| Sivan before landing: "We are doing it for the first time, so that is why we call it 15 minutes of terror" | [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.in/chandrayaan-2s-moment-reckoning-vikram-lander-faces-15-minutes-terror-804893), [Raw Story (AFP)](https://www.rawstory.com/2019/09/15-minutes-of-terror-india-loses-contact-with-moon-lander/) |
| 7 Sep 2019 ~01:50 IST: descent "normal up to an altitude of 2.1 km", then contact lost (Sivan, at mission control) | [Space.com](https://www.space.com/india-loses-contact-with-vikram-moon-lander-chandrayaan-2.html), [Science News](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/india-lost-contact-chandrayaan-lunar-lander-before-touchdown-moon) |
| Modi at ISTRAC Bengaluru: "What we achieved is not small"; next morning Sivan broke down and Modi hugged and consoled him | [Business Standard (PTI)](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/pm-modi-gives-tight-hug-to-emotional-isro-chief-119090700203_1.html), [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/936552/scroll_in) |
| Cause: ISRO (Nov 2019): "the reduction in velocity was more than the designed value"; Sivan (2023): engines commanded to half thrust gave more than half | [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2019/11/26/782890646/2-months-after-failed-moon-landing-india-admits-its-craft-crashed), [arXiv summary](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.14384) |
| Shanmuga Subramanian, Chennai engineer, compared LRO before/after images (17 Sep 2019 image) in his spare time, spotted one bright pixel of debris ~750 m from the impact site, emailed NASA; NASA credited him on 2 Dec 2019 | [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/vikram-lander-found/), [LROC](https://www.lroc.asu.edu/images/1131), [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/945645/scroll_in) |
| Orbiter planned for 1 year, expected to last ~7 (Minister Jitendra Singh, 2021); still working | [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2021/03/envisaged-one-year-chandrayaan-2-orbiter-expected-live-7-years), [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/tech-and-auto/chandrayaan-2-orbiter-likely-to-last-for-seven-years-minister) |
| 21 Aug 2023: ISRO posts "'Welcome, buddy!' Ch-2 orbiter formally welcomed Ch-3 LM. Two-way communication between the two is established." Chandrayaan-3's lander (also named Vikram) landed 23 Aug 2023, 18:04 IST, near the south pole | [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/india/welcome-buddy-chandrayaan-2-orbiter-greets-chandrayaan-3-lander-as-they-establish-communication-1503214117.html), [News on AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/chandrayaan-3-lander-module-vikram-established-communication-with-chandrayaan-2-orbiter-isro) |
