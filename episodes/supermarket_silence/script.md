# Script: "A Toy Starts Talking. They Heard It."

Pitch C (One Night, One Sound). Narrator: Charon, low, tense and intimate. YouTube Short, 9:16, target 50–58 s.
**N** = narration, **TOY** = the toy's voice (also TTS, a different voice, made tinny),
**V** = the visual beat.

| # | Audio | Visual beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **TOY:** "Let's play!" | Close on a little toy robot on a dark shelf; its eyes light up. |
| 2 | **N:** "Three A.M. Aisle six. And a toy just said: let's play." | Hard cut outside: through the storefront glass, the whole horde turns toward the store at once. |
| 3 | **N:** "They're blind. They hunt by sound. And between you and three hundred of them... there's only glass." | Wide inside: you crouched behind a checkout; the horde pressed against the windows, lit by the parking-lot lights. |
| 4 | **N:** "The toy is forty meters away. On this tile, every step is a gunshot." | Low angle down the long main aisle; the faint glow of aisle 6 far away. |
| 5 | **N:** "So you take off your shoes." | You, crouched, start sneaking up the main aisle. |
| 6 | **TOY:** "Let's play!" | The toy's eyes flash again. |
| 7 | **N:** "Aisle four. Aisle five. Behind you... the glass starts to creak." | Tracking you past the aisle ends; cut to zombies clawing at the glass. |
| 8 | **N:** "Aisle six. You grab it, flip it over... and pull the batteries." | You reach the toy shelf and reach out; the toy's eyes go dark. |
| 9 | **N:** "Silence." | Wide, still store. Outside, the horde stops moving. Hold for a beat. |
| 10 | **N:** "Seven hours. That's how long you've survived in here. Seven hours of perfect silence." | You slide down to sit against the shelf and breathe out. |
| 11 | **TOY (all of them):** "LET'S PLAY!" | Every toy on the shelf lights up at once. Camera pulls back. The horde surges at the glass. |
| 12 | **N:** "You'd survive seven hours. The toys had other plans." | Hold on the wall of glowing eyes, then cut to black. |

## Critic pass

- **Hook:** the first second has a sound and an image (the toy's eyes). The second line explains it and
  shows the threat. ✔
- **Clarity:** the rule (they're blind, they hunt by sound) is stated by about 6 s. ✔
- **Escalation:** distance → taking off your shoes → the second chirp → the glass creaking → reaching it. Each beat
  raises the cost of a sound. ✔
- **Payoff:** "Silence" is a false relief; the twist uses the same toy, multiplied. It answers the
  title question ("seven hours") in the last line. ✔
- **Pacing:** about 105 words of narration at a slow delivery ≈ 45–50 s, plus 3 toy lines and the
  "Silence" pause ≈ 55 s. The shortest beats are under 2 s, which suits Shorts.

## Sound (no dialogue besides the toy)

- An ambient bed: fluorescent hum and a low drone that swells as you get closer.
- Zombie murmur outside; it rises on each toy line and dies on "Silence".
- Glass creak on line 7. A sting on line 11.
