# Pitch: "What if you were stuck in a supermarket with sound-sensitive zombies outside? How long would you survive?"

Format: YouTube Short, 9:16, 45–60 s, second-person narration. All three answer the title question
with a number, because "how long" is the promise in the title.

## A. The Survival Clock (recommended)

- **Title:** You're Trapped in a Supermarket. They Can Only Hear You.
- **Hook (0–3 s):** a can rolls off a shelf in a silent store, and 300 heads outside snap toward the glass.
  "Make one sound, and you die. How long can you stay quiet?"
- **Escalation:** on-screen time stamps.
  - **Minute 1:** you learn the rule.
  - **Day 1:** endless food, but every wrapper crinkles. You eat bread, in silence.
  - **Day 3:** the freezers' hum covers your footsteps, so you live beside them.
  - **Day 9:** the power dies. The hum stops. Now even your breathing is loud.
  - **Day 20:** rain, the one day you can move freely.
- **Ending (Day 41):** you never make a sound. But the backup generator kicks in, and the store speakers
  blare *"Attention, shoppers!"* The glass gives way.
  "You'd last 41 days. Not because you made a sound. Because the store did."
- **Why it works:** the counter is a built-in retention device ("what happens on day 41?"). Each step adds
  a new rule about sound, and the twist pays off the premise without blaming the viewer.
- **Engine needs:** a supermarket set, a horde at the windows, day/night/rain looks within one episode
  (per-shot looks), time-stamp captions.

## B. The Rules of Silence

- **Title:** 5 Rules to Survive a Silent Zombie Supermarket.
- **Hook:** "Rule one: take off your shoes. Right now."
- **Escalation:** rules learned the hard way. Shoes off (they squeak on tile). No cans, bread only. Stay by
  the freezer hum. Only move when it rains. Never touch the toy aisle.
- **Ending:** you keep every rule. On day 12 the silence is total, and they can hear the one thing you
  can't stop: your heartbeat. "Day 12."
- **Why it works:** list format, very rewatchable, easy to make a series ("Rules of the Silent Mall").
- **Engine needs:** supermarket set, horde, a couple of looks.

## C. One Night, One Sound

- **Title:** A Toy Starts Talking. They Heard It.
- **Hook:** at 3 AM, in aisle 6, a toy chirps *"Let's play!"* Every zombie outside turns.
- **Escalation:** real time. You cross the dark store to silence it while the horde presses the glass and
  the glass creaks.
- **Ending:** you reach it and pull the batteries. Silence. Then every toy on the shelf lights up at once.
  "You'd survive 7 hours. The toys had other plans."
- **Why it works:** pure tension, one location, one look (cheapest to make).
- **Engine needs:** supermarket set, horde, a night look, a glowing toy prop.
