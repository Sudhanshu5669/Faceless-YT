# "Who is Doctor Doom, and why is Iron Man playing him?": script v1.1 (v1 approved 2026-11-24; v1.1 adds ten lines for length, option 1 chosen by the user)

Format: long-form, 16:9, ~9-10 min, English narration (Charon), four chapters + cold open + bookend. Documentary
storyteller: gripping, dry wit, never mocking the real-world case in chapter 1 (restraint: headlines, no gags about
the crime). Each numbered line becomes one or two shots; *[picture]* notes are the planned scenes, not narration.
Demo shots already approved are marked (demo).

## Script

**COLD OPEN**

1. July 2024. Hall H, San Diego Comic-Con. *[hall_wide (demo)]*
2. More than six thousand fans watch a line of masked men in green cloaks walk onto the stage. *[hall_walk (demo)]*
3. Then one of them takes off his mask. *[hall_unmask (demo)]*
4. It's Robert Downey Junior. Iron Man himself. *[hall_reveal (demo)]*
5. And he tells the crowd: new mask, same task. *[hall_quote (demo)]*
6. The man who spent eleven years as Marvel's greatest hero, who died on screen saving the universe, is coming back
   as the villain who wants to rule it. *[a memorial stand: the red-and-gold helmet on a pedestal in a dark gallery;
   the camera circles; the light goes green and the helmet's shadow on the wall becomes Doom's mask]*
7. His name is Victor von Doom. And to understand why Marvel made this bet, you need two stories. *[the shadow mask
   turns to the lens; title card (HyperFrames): WHO IS DOCTOR DOOM?]*
8. The story of the most dangerous man in Marvel Comics. And the story of the year Marvel's master plan fell apart.
   *[a comic book slaps onto a desk on "Comics"; the Kang plan wall cracks on "fell apart"]*

8a. One is sixty years old. The other is still happening. And they're about to collide on December 18th.
    *[the comic and the wall side by side; a cinema ticket for 18 DEC drops between them]*

**CHAPTER 1: THE VILLAIN MARVEL LOST** *[chapter card]*

9. Rewind to 2019. Avengers: Endgame closes out eleven years of Marvel movies, and makes nearly two point eight
   billion dollars. *[a cinema at midnight, packed; a box-office board rolls; counter lands on $2.8B]*
10. For a while, it's the biggest movie ever made. *[the counter tops a stack of film cans]*
11. Now Marvel needs a new villain. Someone bigger than Thanos. *[the boardroom: Feige pins a blank card in the middle
    of an empty cork wall]*
12. They choose Kang the Conqueror: a man who rules across time itself. *[the blank card flips: THE KANG DYNASTY]*
13. A version of him appears in Loki in 2021. Kang himself arrives in Quantumania in 2023. *[title cards pinned one by
    one, red string pulled taut to the centre on each title]*
14. And at Comic-Con 2022, Marvel announces the movie it's all leading to: Avengers: The Kang Dynasty, set for May
    2025. *[Hall H 2022: the title on the giant screen; the crowd roars; a calendar page pins under it: MAY 2025]*
15. Marvel had built its next saga around one villain: Kang. *[board_plan (demo)]*
16. Then it starts to crack. Quantumania opens big, then fades fast, with brutal reviews.
    *[a box-office line chart falls off a cliff on a monitor in the boardroom]*
17. In March 2023, the actor playing Kang, Jonathan Majors, is arrested in New York.
    *[a news ticker card (HyperFrames) over the boardroom window at night; Feige stops pinning]*
18. Then, in December 2023, the actor who played him is convicted of assault and harassment. *[board_news (demo)]*
19. Marvel drops him the same day. And the whole plan comes down. *[board_collapse (demo)]*
20. Years of story, and no villain at the centre. *[the bare cork wall; one red string swinging; Feige alone]*

**CHAPTER 2: VICTOR** *[chapter card]*

21. Marvel needed someone new. But the answer was already sixty years old. *[a comic shop at night: a hand pulls a
    sealed back issue from a long box: Fantastic Four #5]*
22. July 1962. Stan Lee and Jack Kirby introduce a new villain in Fantastic Four number five. Meet... Doctor Doom.
    *[the real cover, held, tilting into the light on "Meet"]*
22a. In his very first story, Doom kidnaps the Invisible Woman, and forces the rest of the team into his time machine,
    to steal Blackbeard's treasure for him. *[Doom's castle lab: three heroes shoved onto a glowing time platform; a
    pirate ship in a storm; a chest of green gems hauled aboard]*
22b. The Thing ends up becoming Blackbeard. *[the Thing in a pirate hat at the wheel, shrugging; a gull lands on him]*
22c. That's Doom from day one. He doesn't just fight heroes. He makes them work for him. *[the chest set at Doom's
    feet; he doesn't look down]*
22d. Within ten years, he's fought the Avengers, Spider-Man, Iron Man and Daredevil. *[four comic covers slap down one
    per name, a stack growing]*
23. His origin comes two years later. Victor von Doom is born in Latveria, a tiny kingdom in the mountains of
    Europe, to a Romani family. *[dawn over a snowy Alpine valley; a castle on a crag; a camp of painted wagons
    below]*
24. His mother, Cynthia, is a witch. She dies when he's a baby. *[a wagon interior by candle: a cradle; a green
    spark fades from a woman's hand]*
25. His father, Werner, is a healer. When he can't save the Baron's dying wife, he runs with his son into the snow,
    and freezes to death. *[night, a blizzard: the father wraps the boy in his coat under a tree; dawn: only the boy
    stands]*
26. Alone, Victor finds his mother's secret: a chest of magic. And he swears revenge. *[a carved chest creaks open:
    green light on the boy's face; his eyes narrow]*
27. He grows up brilliant. So brilliant, a university in America offers him a scholarship. *[a letter on a crate;
    the boy, now a young man, on a ship's deck as New York rises out of fog]*
28. At Empire State University, Victor builds a machine to reach the realm of the dead, and speak to his mother.
    *[lab_machine (demo)]*
29. His classmate, Reed Richards, spots a flaw in the calculations. *[lab_warning (demo)]*
30. Victor ignores him. *[lab_ignore (demo)]*
31. The machine explodes in his face. *[lab_blast (demo)]*
32. He's expelled. And he blames Reed, forever. *[a rain-wet campus at night: Victor, bandaged, walks out through the
    gates; Reed watches from a window]*
33. In the first comics, the scar is tiny: Jack Kirby drew a small mark on his cheek. But to Victor, his face is
    ruined. *[a mirror in a cheap room: a small scar; the reflection warps bigger and bigger]*
34. He travels to Tibet, where monks teach him their secrets, and there, he forges his armor.
    *[a monastery on a cliff in snow; a forge glowing; hammer blows ring; armor plates on an anvil]*
35. In one famous retelling, he puts on the mask while it's still red-hot. *[tongs lift a glowing mask; it presses
    to the face; steam; the screen whites out]*
36. Victor goes home, and takes the throne of Latveria. *[the castle gates swing open; Victor in full armor walks a
    torchlit hall of kneeling guards]*
37. From that day, he has one name. Doctor Doom. *[the throne: he sits; the camera rises to the mask; thunder]*

**CHAPTER 3: THE MOST DANGEROUS MAN IN MARVEL** *[chapter card]*

38. So why is Doom such a big deal? Start with this: he's a king. *[Castle Doom throne room, grand and wide]*
39. A head of state, with diplomatic immunity. The heroes can't just arrest him. *[at the castle gate, a hero waves
    handcuffs; a Latverian flag drops in front of him; the gate slams]*
40. He's one of the smartest men on Earth. And a sorcerer. *[Doom's gauntlets: one cradles a glowing reactor, the
    other a twisting green spell]*
41. He builds robot copies of himself, Doombots, so you never know if you're fighting the real one.
    *[a row of identical Dooms; one's head pops off in sparks: wires]*
42. And every year, he fights the Devil himself, for his mother's soul. *[a red-lit cavern: Doom versus a horned
    shadow, sparks of green and red]*
43. He once stole the power of the Silver Surfer. *[a silver board crackles with power; Doom rises into the sky on
    it]*
44. In 1984's Secret Wars, he steals the power of the Beyonder, a being so powerful he's practically a god. For a
    moment, Doom holds the universe in his hand. *[a white-gold sphere of power drawn into Doom's open gauntlet]*
45. In 2015, he goes even further. When the multiverse ends, Doom saves what's left, stitches it into one patchwork
    world called Battleworld, and rules it as God Emperor Doom. *[a planet of mismatched continents; a gold throne
    on a world tree; Doom in white and gold]*
45b. And at the very end, Reed Richards does what Victor never could. He heals his face. *[a white-gold light; the
    mask lifted away by a stretched hand; we see only Victor's eyes widen, the face itself kept off screen]*
45a. And in 2025, after taking the title of Sorcerer Supreme from Doctor Strange, he does it again. Overnight, Doom
    takes over the whole world, and names himself its emperor. *[a cloak and amulet settle onto Doom's shoulders; the
    camera pulls out from Latveria to a globe of green-lit cities, banners with his mask unrolling on every skyline]*
46. That's the story Marvel's next two Avengers movies are building toward. *[the two titles on the board: DOOMSDAY,
    SECRET WARS]*
47. And here's the twist. In 2016, while Tony Stark lay in a coma, Doom put on the armor, and became Iron Man.
    *[a hospital bed with Tony's helmet on the table; a gauntlet lifts it; the reflection in its eyes is Doom]*
48. Doom has already been Iron Man once. *[Doom in a red-and-grey suit, against a city at night]*

**CHAPTER 4: WHY IRON MAN** *[chapter card]*

49. So why Robert Downey Junior? *[Hall H again, the masked figures, quieter]*
49a. Four months before Hall H, he'd just won his first Oscar, for Oppenheimer. *[a dressing room: the golden statue
    on the table beside the folded green cloak]*
49b. And Doom has been on the big screen before. Julian McMahon played him twice for Fox, in 2005 and 2007. Toby
    Kebbell played him in 2015. *[three film-strip frames slide past: three masks of different designs, rough
    silhouettes]*
49c. Fans have waited a long time for a Doom that feels like the comics. *[the comic cover again, beside the masked
    figure on stage]*
50. Because Doom is Tony Stark's dark mirror. Both are geniuses. Both hide inside metal suits. Both have enormous
    egos. *[two pedestals in the dark gallery: the helmet and the mask, turning in sync, three beats on the three
    "Both"s]*
51. Tony learned to use his for other people. Victor never did. *[warm light falls on the helmet; cold green on the
    mask]*
52. Marvel brought back the directors who made Endgame, Joe and Anthony Russo. *[the Russos on the Hall H stage]*
53. And according to Variety, Downey's deal is worth more than eighty million dollars. *[a contract slides across
    the boardroom table; counter lands on $80,000,000+]*
53a. But how can the X-Men and the Fantastic Four even be in an Avengers movie? In 2019, Disney bought Twenty-First
    Century Fox, and with it, the film rights to both. *[a gavel; two film-reel cans slide across the boardroom table
    to Feige]*
53b. In 2025, The Fantastic Four: First Steps brought Marvel's first family home. And Doom comes with them. *[a retro
    rocket on a launch pad at dawn; its shadow on the ground is the shape of a mask]*
54. The trailers show the X-Men from the old Fox movies, the Fantastic Four, and Steve Rogers, back. *[the cinema:
    trailer light flickering on a delighted audience]*
55. And in the trailer, Sue Storm says it simply: he used to be different. *[the mask in close-up; one green eye moves]*
56. Avengers: Doomsday arrives on December 18th. Secret Wars follows in December 2027. *[the cinema marquee: dates
    on the board]*
57. Whatever Victor von Doom wants this time, one thing is certain. He has never settled for saving the world.
    *[Castle Doom at night, lightning; the camera pushes into the lit window]*

**BOOKEND**

58. Back in Hall H, the man who played Iron Man lifts the mask one more time. *[RDJ raises the mask to his face]*
59. New mask. Same task. *[the mask closes; the screens flare green; cut to black]*

About 1055 narration words: ~7.5 min of voice at the demo pace (141 wpm), plus four chapter cards, held payoffs and the music's breathing room: about 8.5-9 min.
